Reviewed factual summary, not a verbatim copy of the official webpage.
Graduate admissions: the application charge is USD 60 and cannot be refunded.
The purpose statement has a requested length of 500 to 1,000 words.
Most graduate degrees ask for two academic or professional references; some doctoral degrees ask for three. Personal references are not accepted.
Applicants can initially upload unofficial transcripts, but official versions are due before classes begin. Every attended postsecondary institution must supply an official transcript, including institutions where no degree was earned.
